Migration vs. Life Expectancy

Do countries with higher life expectancy attract more immigrants?

Story: Prosperity Attracts People

This scatter plot shows that countries with higher life expectancy—often proxies for better healthcare, safety, and living standards—tend to have positive net migration rates. In contrast, countries with shorter life expectancy frequently experience population outflows, as citizens move abroad seeking better conditions.
