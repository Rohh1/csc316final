Story: Who is Gaining and Losing People?

This choropleth uses a diverging blue–red color scale to show which countries are net immigration magnets (deep blue) and which are experiencing net emigration (deep red). Larger countries appear slightly thicker because their borders are drawn with a heavier outline.

Try filtering by region and zooming into clusters (for example, Western Europe or the Middle East) to see how neighboring countries can have very different migration profiles despite sharing geography.

Democratic Gravity Through Regions

This view shows how democracy clusters and shifts over time. Each colored circle is a regional “orbit,” summarizing the countries within that region. The X-axis shows neighbors’ electoral democracy and the Y-axis shows neighbors’ overall democracy level. Circle size represents the total number of members in the region’s parliaments, hinting at the scale of its representative institutions.

You can click a region to drill down and see individual countries as separate “planets.” Use the year slider or Play button to move from 1950–2020. Faint trails trace each region’s or country’s historical path.
