Story: Migration Flows Reflect Economic Opportunity

Countries in **North America and Western Europe** generally show positive net migration rates, meaning they attract more immigrants than they lose citizens. This trend reflects strong labor markets, political stability, and high living standards.

In contrast, many **developing economies** in Africa, Asia, and Latin America have negative net migration rates, as citizens seek better opportunities abroad. The global map illustrates this divide clearly — regions with higher income and stability consistently attract population inflows.
