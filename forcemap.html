Story: Prosperity as Gravity

Circles are countries (size = population). Blue glow indicates net immigration; red glow indicates net emigration. A gentle “pull” toward the global attractor (derived from countries with top life expectancy) visualizes how health and prosperity draw people, while countries with lower life expectancy drift outward.
