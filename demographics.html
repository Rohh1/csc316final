Visualizing the components of population change: Natural Change vs. Net Migration.

Story: Dual Drivers of Population Change

This stacked bar chart breaks down a country's total population growth rate (length of the bar) into its two major components: the Natural Change Rate (Births - Deaths) and the Net Migration Rate.

The chart reveals that population stability in Developed Economies is often heavily reliant on Net Immigration (blue segments) to offset low or negative natural change. Conversely, for many Developing Economies, growth is driven almost entirely by the Natural Change Rate (green/brown segments), even if they experience significant net emigration.
