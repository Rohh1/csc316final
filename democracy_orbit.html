Democratic Gravity Through Regions

This view shows how democracy clusters and shifts over time. Each colored circle is a regional “orbit,” summarizing the countries within that region. In the regional view, the X-axis shows neighbors’ electoral democracy (further right = more democratic elections) and the Y-axis shows neighbors’ overall democracy level (higher up = more democratic systems overall). Circle size represents the total number of members in the region’s parliaments, hinting at the scale of its representative institutions.

You can click a region to drill down and see individual countries as separate “planets,” still positioned by their democratic context and sized by their parliamentary seats. Use the year slider or Play button to move from 1950–2020. Faint trails trace each region’s or country’s historical path, revealing how they drift toward or away from their neighbors’ democratic norms over time—whether clustering into stable democratic neighborhoods or sliding into more authoritarian gravitational fields.
