Story: Democratic Gravity

Each country is positioned based on its neighbors' average democratic characteristics. The X-axis shows neighbors' electoral index (right = more democratic elections). The Y-axis shows neighbors' democracy level (up = more democratic neighbors).

Planet size represents parliament members. Yellow rings indicate proportional voting systems. Trails show historical paths, revealing how countries drift toward or away from their neighbors' democratic norms over decades.
