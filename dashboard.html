Story: Linking Health and Demographic Balance (with Group Mean Analysis)

Countries with high life expectancy and low infant mortality typically maintain balanced birth and death rates — indicators of stable, developed populations. Meanwhile, nations with high infant mortality often show very high birth rates, reflecting younger demographic structures and faster population growth.

**Novelty:** Use the brushing tool on one scatter plot to select a group of countries. The same points will highlight and fade in the other plot. A **gold diamond (the Group Mean)** will appear on *both* plots, showing the average demographic and health profile of the selected group. This quickly reveals how the overall trend of a specific cluster (e.g., high infant mortality nations) compares across both health and demographic variables simultaneously.
