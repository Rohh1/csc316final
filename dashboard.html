Population Health Dashboard

Explore how life expectancy, infant mortality, birth rates, and death rates combine to paint different health profiles around the world.

Story: From Survival to Stability

Showing all countries – hover, brush, or filter to see how health profiles shift.

On the left, each medical cross represents a country. Moving right means higher life expectancy, while moving up means higher infant mortality. The color shows a data-driven health profile:

  • Health leaders – long lives, low infant mortality
  • In transition – improving but mixed outcomes
  • High risk – shorter lives and higher infant deaths

On the right, the same countries are pulled into four birth–death quadrants using a force layout:

  • Fast-growing – high birth, low death
  • High churn – high birth, high death
  • Stable – low birth, low death
  • Aging & pressured – low birth, high death

Brush a cluster in the left panel: those countries glow as medical crosses inside their quadrant, revealing how survival and demographic momentum interact for different health profiles.
