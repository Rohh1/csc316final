Population Health Dashboard

Explore how life expectancy, infant mortality, birth rates, and death rates work together to shape population health around the world.

Story: From Survival to Stability

Showing all countries – hover, brush, or filter to see how patterns change.

In the left panel, each circle is a country. Moving right means higher life expectancy, while moving up means higher infant mortality. Countries with long lives and low infant deaths cluster in the lower-right – the “health leaders.”

On the right, the same countries are pulled into four birth–death quadrants using a force layout:

  • Fast-growing – high birth, low death
  • High churn – high birth, high death
  • Stable – low birth, low death
  • Aging & pressured – low birth, high death

Brush a cluster in the life–infant scatter: the same countries glow inside their quadrant, revealing how survival and demographic momentum interact.
